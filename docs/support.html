Pickly Support

Pickly helps you scan, search, compare, and save grocery products.

Before contacting support

  • Check that Pickly has camera access if barcode scanning is unavailable.
  • Use manual barcode entry or search when a barcode cannot be read.
  • Use Restore Purchases on the Pickly Plus screen if an active subscription is not recognized.
  • Account access is optional. Account deletion is available from Profile → Account.

Report a problem

Include the affected screen, your iOS version, and the steps that reproduce the issue. Do not include passwords, authentication codes, or payment information.

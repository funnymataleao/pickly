Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

What Pickly Uses

Pickly uses the camera only after you start barcode scanning. Camera frames are processed for barcode recognition and are not stored by the MVP.

A barcode may be sent to Open Food Facts to look up product information. Open Food Facts may receive the barcode and the request metadata needed to provide the response.

If you create an account, Pickly processes your email address for sign-in. The MVP keeps saved products, history, and preferences on this device.

Your Choices

Camera access is optional. You can search the sample catalog or enter a barcode manually instead.

You can delete your Pickly account from the Account screen. Deleting the account also clears local saved products and preferences.

Data Retention

Local saved products, history, and preferences remain on this device until you remove them, delete the app, or delete your account from Pickly.

This MVP does not use advertising identifiers, location, contacts, or a social profile.

Data Collection Summary

Pickly collects and processes the following data:

  • Email Address: Used for account creation and sign-in (optional)
  • Product Barcodes: Sent to Open Food Facts for product lookup
  • Saved Products: Stored locally on your device
  • Preferences: Stored locally on your device

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

Pickly uses the following third-party services:

  • Open Food Facts: Product information lookup service
  • Supabase: Backend infrastructure for optional account features
  • Google Sign-In: Optional authentication method
  • Sign in with Apple: Optional authentication method

These services have their own privacy policies governing the use of your information.

Children's Privacy

Pickly is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the app's support section or via our GitHub repository.
